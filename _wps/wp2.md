**Work Package 2 (WP2)** uses the patient data collected in WP1 to build computer simulations that predict how the knee joint and implant behave inside the body.
To evaluate mechanical stress, wear, and stability, the team is focusing on three main steps:

• Simulating body movement: Creating personalized virtual models of the patient's bones, muscles, and ligaments to measure joint forces and detect signs of instability or pain during daily activities.

• Testing implant wear in the lab: Running physical stress tests on the knee implant using a special fluid that mimics natural joint lubrication to see how it wears down under different pressures.

• Speeding up predictions: Combining physical test results, blood inflammation markers, and X-ray data into fast "meta-models" that quickly forecast implant wear and pressure for future patients.
