**Work Package 4 (WP4)** brings everything together by combining the data and simulations from the first three work packages to build the final, patient-specific Digital Twin.
To turn complex multi-scale research into a practical predictive tool, the team is focusing on three main steps:

• Harmonizing the modeling tools: Selecting shared software frameworks and training all partners to account for natural variations and uncertainties across different types of medical and simulation data.

• Finding key risk patterns: Merging clinical scores, biomarkers, and mechanical stress data to spot trends across patient groups and identify the strongest warning signs for complications.

• Building the Digital Twin: Creating a streamlined virtual model that predicts a patient's risk of postoperative dissatisfaction—working both for fully monitored new patients and past patients with standard medical records.
