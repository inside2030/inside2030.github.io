**Work Package 1 (WP1)** focuses on gathering real-world clinical and movement data from knee revision patients to predict their recovery outcomes.
To build and train the project's virtual model, the team is following a three-step approach:

• Tracking new patients: Following 30 high-risk patients undergoing knee revision surgery using medical scans, blood tests, psychological assessments, and camera-based movement analysis during daily activities.

• Learning from past cases: Analyzing existing medical records from 100 previous patients to quickly build an initial version of the virtual model.

• Predicting recovery: Combining physical movement data with clinical details like pain and mental health to forecast potential patient dissatisfaction early on.
